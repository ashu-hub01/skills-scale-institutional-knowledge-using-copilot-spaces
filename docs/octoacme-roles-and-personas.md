# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Release Manager

### Role Summary
Release Managers oversee the planning, coordination, and execution of software releases. They ensure that deployments are smooth, well-coordinated across teams, and aligned with business objectives.

### Responsibilities
- Plan and schedule release windows in coordination with stakeholders
- Coordinate deployment activities across development, QA, and operations teams
- Maintain and execute release checklists and runbooks
- Manage rollback procedures and incident response during deployments
- Track release notes, documentation, and communication timelines
- Monitor post-deployment verification and status dashboards
- Serve as the central point of escalation for release-related issues

### Goals
- Minimize deployment risk and time-to-recovery from issues
- Ensure clear communication and alignment across all teams during releases
- Establish repeatable, efficient release processes
- Reduce mean time to deployment (MTPD) and mean time to recovery (MTTR)

### Typical Communication
- Release planning meetings and stakeholder sync
- Pre-deployment and post-deployment status updates
- Incident communication and rollback coordination
- Release notes and deployment documentation

### Interaction with Other Roles
- **Developers**: coordinate feature readiness and deployment dependencies
- **QA Lead**: ensure testing is complete and release criteria are met
- **Project Manager**: align on timeline and stakeholder communication
- **Operations**: coordinate deployment execution and monitoring

---

## Quality Assurance Lead

### Role Summary
QA Leads own the quality strategy, testing methodology, and acceptance criteria validation. They ensure that features meet quality standards before release and maintain testing infrastructure and best practices.

### Responsibilities
- Define and maintain quality standards and acceptance criteria for all features
- Plan and execute testing strategy (unit, integration, end-to-end, performance)
- Manage QA workflows and testing tools and infrastructure
- Identify and track quality metrics and defect trends
- Coordinate with developers on test coverage and quality gates
- Validate release readiness and sign-off on quality metrics
- Document testing procedures and maintain test case libraries
- Drive continuous improvement in testing processes

### Goals
- Prevent critical defects from reaching production
- Maintain high test coverage and reduce escape rate
- Enable faster, more confident releases
- Establish testing as a core part of the development workflow

### Typical Communication
- Quality gates and acceptance criteria discussions
- Test status and defect metrics in weekly syncs
- Release readiness validation and sign-off
- Process improvement recommendations in retrospectives

### Interaction with Other Roles
- **Developers**: collaborate on test design, automation, and quality gates
- **Release Manager**: validate release readiness and coordinate testing timelines
- **Product Manager**: clarify acceptance criteria and success metrics
- **Project Manager**: provide testing schedule and risk assessment

---

## Technical Writer

### Role Summary
Technical Writers create, maintain, and improve documentation that helps teams, users, and stakeholders understand processes, features, and systems. They ensure clarity and accessibility of information across the organization.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project documentation, process guides, and runbooks
- Document technical architecture, APIs, and system design
- Maintain user-facing documentation and help resources
- Ensure consistency in documentation style and terminology across projects
- Gather information from subject matter experts and translate into clear guidance
- Review and update documentation during retrospectives and process improvements
- Manage documentation tooling and knowledge base infrastructure

### Goals
- Reduce onboarding time and knowledge gaps for new team members
- Enable self-service support and reduce ad-hoc questions
- Capture and scale institutional knowledge
- Maintain a single source of truth for processes and systems

### Typical Communication
- Documentation planning and information gathering sessions
- Regular updates to process docs during retros and improvements
- Knowledge base maintenance and quality reviews
- Coordination with Project Managers and Subject Matter Experts

### Interaction with Other Roles
- **Project Managers**: document processes and capture learnings from projects
- **Developers**: document technical specifications and architecture decisions
- **QA Lead**: document testing procedures and quality standards
- **All roles**: gather input and feedback on documentation clarity
